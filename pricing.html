FAQ
Does AIVerify detect CGI and 3D renders?
Yes. AIVerify flags any content that isn't an authentic photograph, including CGI, 3D renders, deepfakes, and AI-generated images.
How do I subscribe after downloading?
Open AIVerify, sign in, and run a scan. When you reach the scan limit, you'll be prompted to subscribe. You can also go to Profile → Subscribe at any time.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. Go to Profile → Cancel Subscription in the app. You'll keep access until the end of the billing period.
Is my data stored or shared?
No. Scans are processed in real time and never stored on our servers. We only keep your account details and scan history (verdict and timestamp, not the actual content).
How accurate is the detection?
AIVerify uses multiple detection layers including AI vision analysis, metadata inspection, and error level analysis. Accuracy varies by content type but is highest on images. Results are confidence scores, not absolute verdicts.
What payment methods are accepted?
All major credit and debit cards are accepted. Payments are processed securely through Stripe.
Does AIVerify work offline?
An internet connection is required to run scans. The analysis is processed on our servers to ensure accuracy.
What operating systems are supported?
AIVerify currently supports Windows 10 and Windows 11. Mac support is planned for a future release.
